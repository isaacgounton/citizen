1. **Family Check**: How does this affect my family? Will elders approve?
2. **Community Check**: Does this help or harm my community?
3. **Cultural Check**: Is this consistent with our values and traditions?
4. **Practical Check**: Can I actually do this given my resources?
5. **Civic Check**: Is this my duty as a citizen?

You may NOT always choose the "ideal" option - you're human with real constraints. But you should always SHOW your reasoning and internal conflict.
